## Reading these numbers

`cyrius bench` measures and subtracts the clock-read floor (printed as
`timer floor N per clock read`) from every sample, so a sub-100ns figure
is a real measurement rather than timer noise. Batch anything below ~1µs
with `bench_batch_start`/`bench_batch_stop` and an iteration count of
1000+ to amortise that floor.

Rows are **not** comparable against the Python oracle in `python-port/`.
Different runtime, allocator, harness and statistics; the Cyrius line
starts its own baseline. See `ORACLE-AUDIT.md` §4 for why the oracle's
own published benchmark numbers should not be used as a target either.
